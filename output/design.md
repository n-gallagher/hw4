# Campus Customs — Design (Problem 10)

**Direction: "Varsity Heritage."** A century-old campus shop reimagined: Yale navy + old gold + warm ivory paper, collegiate block lettering, stitched and chenille-patch details. The goal is a site that feels like *the* Yale store, not a template, and turns browsing into buying with fewer clicks.

| Area | What we changed | Why it helps customers stick around and buy |
|---|---|---|
| **Fonts** | **Graduate** (a collegiate varsity-letter typeface) for headlines and prices; **Inter** for body text. Both are self-hosted, so pages don't wait on Google. | Instantly reads "Yale athletics" and sets the brand apart. Inter keeps long text and prices easy to scan. |
| **Color & texture** | Navy `#00356b`, old gold `#c9a227`, ivory paper `#f7f2e7` with a faint grain. Gold is reserved for actions (Shop, Add, Send) and highlights. | Warm and premium instead of stark white. A single action color teaches the eye where to click. |
| **Hierarchy** | Uppercase block headlines, gold letter-spaced eyebrows, a tilted **"EST. NEW HAVEN · 57 BROADWAY"** stamp, gold underline on the active nav link, varsity stripe under the nav (gold/white/navy), stitched seam dividers. | Clear reading order (headline → why → button). The heritage cues build trust that this is the official, local shop. |
| **Home: product-led hero** | Three **real products fanned** like cards on a table (they float gently and straighten on hover), a giant faded collegiate "Y" watermark, and a gold **★ Shop the collection** button. | Before, the home page had zero products. Now merchandise is the first thing you see, and every fanned card is one click from its page. |
| **Shop by category "patches"** | Six chenille-patch tiles (navy felt, gold stitching) with **live counts and starting prices** from the database ("27 styles · from $45"). They deep-link to the filtered grid (`/products?category=Hoodies`). | One click to the right aisle, with real numbers that set price expectations up front. |
| **Product presentation** | Cards on ivory with a felt-patch category tag. On hover the **photo zooms**, a **stitched gold outline** appears, and a **Quick add** size row slides up (sold-out sizes crossed out). | Shoppers can add to the bag **without leaving the grid**, which is fewer steps to purchase, and sold-out sizes are never a surprise. |
| **Consistent white photo backgrounds** | 76 of the 102 catalogue photos had black backgrounds or black side bars baked in. `backend/images.py` makes white-background copies: only black *connected to the photo's edge* is filled, so dark navy fabric and black logos are untouched, and a safety check keeps the original if a fill would leak into the garment. Originals are never modified; copies are made once at API start (about 50 s, `data/products_clean/`) and served in place of the originals. Every result was checked by eye on before/after sheets. | A clean, uniform grid looks like a premium store and keeps the eye on the product, not on random black boxes. |
| **Product page** | **Hover magnifier** (the image zooms 2× and follows the cursor), collegiate title and price, gold ring on the selected size. | Shoppers can inspect stitching, logos and fabric up close, which matters most for apparel bought online. |
| **Chat feel: Bulldog concierge** | **Handsome Dan himself is the chat button**: a big bobbing bulldog in the bottom-right corner of every page with a "Woof! Need a paw?" speech bubble; click him and the chat opens right where he sits. Inside: his avatar on the header and every reply, speech-bubble tails, animated **typing dots** with rotating captions; **page-aware quick replies** (on a product page with size M set: "Is this in stock in M?", "Add this in M to my bag"). | A friendly, on-brand helper gets used more than a plain support box. The page-aware chips turn the next question (or the next sale) into one tap. |
| **Handsome Dan vibes** | The concierge is now **Handsome Dan** (Yale's real bulldog mascot): he **blinks and wiggles his ears**, **tilts his head while thinking** ("Sniffing the stockroom…", "Fetching the details…"), is **the chat button on every page** (see Chat feel), leaves a **paw-print trail** across the home page, **stamps paw prints out of the bag icon** on every add, **guards an empty bag** (sleepy Dan with a floating "z"), and looks sad on the 404 page. His voice has at most one light bulldog touch per reply, with facts always first. | Personality makes the brand memorable and the helper inviting to use; a mascot shoppers already love turns "a chatbot" into "Dan," and playful feedback (paw burst on add) rewards the actions that lead to a sale. |
| **Motion** | Page fade-in on every route, sections that **reveal on scroll**, staggered card entrances, button press feedback, bag badge pop, chat panel scale-in. All turned off for "reduce motion" users. Reveals never hide content that's already on screen. | Motion signals "alive and responsive" and guides attention to new content (cards, bag, replies) without slowing anyone down. |
| **Pennant footer** | Gold/white/navy stripe, a tilted navy **pennant**, Visit / Shop / Promise columns (57 Broadway, officially licensed, live prices & stock). | Ends every page on brand and trust, with quick links back into the shop. |

**Files:** `frontend/src/varsity.css` (design layer, loaded last), `pages/Home.tsx`, `components/ProductCard.tsx` (quick add), `components/BulldogAvatar.tsx`, `components/ChatWidget.tsx` (concierge), `components/RevealOnScroll.tsx`, `components/Footer.tsx`, `pages/ProductDetail.tsx` (magnifier), `pages/Products.tsx` (`?category=` deep links, staggered grid).

**Checked in the running app:** Graduate + Inter load; the hero shows 3 real products with images; the patches show live counts (e.g. Hoodies 27 · from $45) and link to `/products?category=Hoodies` (27 cards); quick add put a Brooks Brothers hoodie (S) in the bag without leaving the grid (badge 6 → 7, XS disabled because it's sold out); the concierge shows the avatar and page-aware chips; the magnifier follows the cursor; no console errors.
