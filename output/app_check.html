⚠ Needs revisiting (found during this test)

Testing found 7 issues; all are fixed and re-tested (screenshots in Re-test after fixes).

Show the 7 fixes and 1 note
  1. FIXED & RE-TESTED Usage log lost the list of tools called. In agent.log_usage, the usage summary's numeric tool_calls count overwrote the list of tool calls, so the audit log showed 3 instead of which tools ran. Fixed by storing the list as tools_called; Check 1 was re-run and the log now shows each call.
  2. FIXED & RE-TESTED Fact-checker skipped size-labelled counts. The first run's badge said "1 stock count verified" although the reply also listed "S (15), L (25), XXL (25)". Stock counts written as S (15) / M: 5 weren't recognized. Added that pattern (a test with a wrong "L (99)" is now caught); the re-run shows "4 stock counts verified". The graded question set still passes 8/8 afterwards.
  3. FIXED & RE-TESTED Agent nudged "check out soon", but checkout isn't open yet (the bag shows "Checkout (coming soon)"). Added a prompt rule: never say "check out / buy now"; for low stock, suggest keeping it in the bag and note items aren't reserved until checkout opens. (First-run reply: "There are only 5 left in our system, so you may want to check out soon.") Re-test reply: "Only 5 medium units are left in stock, so it's worth keeping an eye on. Items aren't reserved until checkout opens."
  4. FIXED & RE-TESTED Category answers showed only the first 6 of 29 with no way to see the rest. The results section now adds a "See all 29 crewnecks →" link to /products?category=Crewnecks whenever all matches share a category (count read live from the catalogue).
  5. FIXED & RE-TESTED Bag adds reused the "From your chat" banner (in the first run its title became "PLEASE ADD A MEDIUM…"). Replies that only add to the bag no longer replace the banner; the bag drawer shows the item, and the chat button reads "View your bag →".
  6. FIXED & RE-TESTED Create-account form overflow (reported while reviewing the site): the side-by-side First/Last name fields didn't shrink, so the Last name box spilled past the card's right edge. Fixed in CSS; every input now ends 33 px inside the card, aligned with Email and Password.
  7. FIXED & RE-TESTED Clicking a chat card didn't visibly open the product (found in a final review, 11 Oct 2026). The URL changed, but the "From your chat" section stayed at the top of the page, so the product sat below the fold and the screen looked unchanged. Now every page change starts at the top and, on a product page, the chat results sit below the product, so the item you clicked is the first thing you see (a new question asked on a product page still scrolls its results into view). Screenshot 2b was re-taken through the real flow.
  8. NOTE The fact-checker can raise a false alarm on unusual phrasings like "2 in M-L range"; the effect is only an extra retry, never a wrong answer to the shopper.
